## 1. The shop

Source: https://runescape.wiki/w/Slayer_Equipment

"Slayer Equipment is a shop run by Slayer Masters that consists of various tools and
weapons for use against Slayer monsters. Prices and stock are shared between all Slayer
Masters except those in Burthorpe, which instead use the Slayer equipment shop in
Burthorpe."

Owners (one shop, shared stock): Jacquelyn (Lumbridge, F2P), Vannaka (Edgeville /
Edgeville Dungeon), Mazchna/Achtryn (Canifis), Chaeldar (Zanaris), The Raptor (Fort
Forinthry), Sumona (Pollnivneach), Duradel/Lapalok (Shilo Village), Kuradal (Ancient
Cavern), Morvran (Prifddinas), Laniakea (Anachronia Swamp Fortress / base camp),
Mandrith (Wilderness, F2P). Turael/Spria (Burthorpe) use a separate shop.

Reset note: "Enchanted gem packs are restocked to full. Enchanted gems are unaffected."

## 2. Finishing blows (Slayer Rewards unlock)

Source: https://runescape.wiki/w/Template:Slayer_Rewards_stock

"Learn how to deliver killing blows quicker" costs 400 Slayer points; "(with 75 Slayer)".
"Monsters which require a finishing blow to kill, such as rock slugs, desert lizards,
zygomites, and gargoyles will die when they reach zero life points like normal monsters,
as long as the item required to kill them is in the backpack or tool belt. This ability
functions with ranged and magic attacks; the player does not need to be positioned
adjacent to the monster to activate the finishing blow effect."

History (https://runescape.wiki/w/Slayer_Rewards): 3 March 2010 (Shattered Heart): "The
ability to deal killing blows faster to specific Slayer creatures can now be purchased";
11 January 2016: "Familiars no longer block the 'deliver finishing blows quicker' slayer
ability."

Important: even with the unlock the item is still needed in the backpack or tool belt.

## 3. Items

### Slayer collection log
Source: https://runescape.wiki/w/Slayer_collection_log
- Free, F2P. Config: not found in pan-Obj.txt.
- "a collection log and book that keeps track of assorted drops received from Slayer monsters. It can be obtained from any Slayer Master's shop for free. The interface can also be accessed by right-clicking the Slayer kill counter and choosing the Collection Log option".
- Not needed for any task. Completing each area gives a title.
- Update history: 4 Nov 2024 Spectral lens added to the Kharidian Desert log; 11 Apr 2023 Bone Fragments added (General) and Greater Sonic Wave Ability Codex added (Wilderness); 25 Jul 2022 ectoplasmator fix; 30 May 2022 Jaws of the Abyss link fix; 27 Sep 2021 Senntisten section fix; 6 Apr 2021 logs stay open in combat and update in real time; 6 Apr 2020 logs open correctly via the Slayer counter; 9 Dec 2019 favourite button description; 23 Sep 2019 added to game.

### Enchanted gem
Source: https://runescape.wiki/w/Enchanted_gem
- 1 coin, F2P. Config: `SLAYER_GEM` (4155), `CERT_SLAYER_GEM` (27052).
- "Activating the gem remotely contacts the player's current slayer master." Right-click "Kills-left". "New Slayer assignments cannot be obtained through the gems".
- Alternatives named on the page: charged combat bracelet, and the Slayer counter (Gameplay > Interfaces > Information Windows) which "shows the number of kills left in the current task. Hovering the mouse over the counter identifies the assigned monster and, for players with access to the sunken pyramid, whether the monster's soul has been captured."
- Used for co-op Slayer invites, and as a component of the ring of slaying and Commorb.
- Not needed for any task.
- Update history: 9 Dec 2019 kills-left shown in an infobox; 4 Nov 2019 Turael/Spria location text; 10 Jun 2019 gem contacts Mandrith on a Mandrith task; 26 Mar 2018 gem packs added; 18 May 2015 bank "Kills left" message fix; 11 May 2015 can be equipped in the pocket slot; 13 Apr 2015, 7 Sep 2010, 10 Aug 2009, 22 Jun 2009 small fixes; 26 Jan 2005 added to game.

### Enchanted gem pack
Source: https://runescape.wiki/w/Enchanted_gem_pack
- 75 coins, members. Config: not found.
- "an item pack containing 50 noted enchanted gems ... useful for making rings of slaying in bulk".
- Update history: 3 Apr 2018 Burthorpe shop stocks packs, restock at midnight; 26 Mar 2018 added to game.

### Mirror shield
Source: https://runescape.wiki/w/Mirror_shield
- 5,000 coins, members. Requires 25 Slayer, 20 Defence. Config: `SLAYER_MIRROR_SHIELD` (4156).
- Tasks: cockatrices and basilisks. "While fighting these monsters, the shield must be equipped, or the player's stats will be drastically reduced."
- Alternatives: "A Mask of Stone, Helm of Petrification, Mask of Reflection, Helm of Little Kings or sunglasses can be used to substitute for the shield."
- Assignment rule: "Players must have the requisite Defence and Slayer levels to wear the shield in order to be assigned a creature requiring the shield while fighting it as a Slayer task."
- Required (or a substitute); not a finisher.
- Update history: 16 Feb 2015 now hybrid-class; 25 Feb 2009 Torag clipping fix; 26 Jan 2005 added.

### Leaf-bladed spear
Source: https://runescape.wiki/w/Leaf-bladed_spear
- 31,000 coins, members, untradeable since 22 Jan 2007. Requires 55 Slayer, 50 Attack. Config: `SLAYER_LEAFBLADED_SPEAR` (4158).
- Tasks: turoths and kurasks, "as an alternative to Slayer Dart, a leaf-bladed sword, broad arrows and broad-tipped bolts."
- Required: one of these weapon types is needed for turoth/kurask (exact wording on the turoth/kurask pages not checked: not verified).
- Update history: 22 Sep 2014 two-handed damage fix; 22 Jan 2007 no longer tradeable; 26 Jan 2005 added.

### Broad arrow
Source: https://runescape.wiki/w/Broad_arrow
