# Slayer challenges (RS3 wiki reference)

Source: https://runescape.wiki/w/Slayer/Challenges (read 2026-10-23 through a
page reader: a summary, not verbatim). Licence: RuneScape Wiki text, CC BY-NC-SA 3.0.

- Special assignments a master sometimes offers instead of a task. Bonus Slayer XP
  on completion (not boosted). Points from most masters after Smoking Kills;
  Turael/Spria and Kuradal give no points.
- Counts as an assignment for the 10th/50th point bonus. Repeatable.
- Offered only after 10 completed tasks and when not blocked at a master. Chance
  X/100 per new task (The Raptor X/50), X = consecutive completed tasks; declining
  or cancelling resets X to 0; any challenge resets the odds with all masters.
  Declining costs no points. Whether a challenge counts for the streak: not stated.

| Master | Challenge | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Turael/Spria | kill all monsters in their basement in one visit | 1,000 XP |
| Jacquelyn | defeat Dragith Nurn (Lumbridge Catacombs) | 1,000 XP, 5 points |
| Vannaka | hand in a perfect green, blue, red and black dragon scale (What Lies Below) | 4,000 XP, 10 points |
| The Raptor | 120-149 undead around the fort (zombie 1, skeleton 2, ghost 3, phantom 4) | 3,000 XP, 20 points, trophy |
| Mazchna/Achtryn | summon and kill ravenous monsters at fairy rings CKS, ALQ, BKR | 2,000 XP, 3 points |
| Chaeldar | experimental fungicide on 10 mutated zygomites | 10,000 XP, 25 points |
| Sumona | defeat the Kalphite Queen | 15,000 XP, 30 points |
| Duradel/Lapalok | one Slayer Tower trip: set numbers of 8 monster types | 20,000 XP, 35 points |
| Kuradal | 280 monsters in the TzHaar Fight Cave | 25,000 XP, no points |
| Morvran | kill each listed boss once | 50,000 XP, 45 points; title "The Famous" (comp cape); buyable for 100 points once a week |
| Laniakea | a poison dose from each vile bloom | 50,000 XP, 45 points |
| Mandrith | 15 each of 13 Wilderness monster types (optional bosses) | 21,500-30,000 XP, 35 points |
